## Manufacturing experience and administration

The integrated `/manufacturing` experience presents the planned production journey in six stages, followed by quality assurance, billet references, downstream product categories, infrastructure, responsible production, industries and customer enquiries. `/technology` uses the same experience; `/quality` opens its quality section. The 3D scenes are procedural conceptual illustrations, not engineering models or evidence of installed facilities. The content is loaded from the server's published version with a reviewed reference-content fallback.

Authorized administrators can edit and preview a manufacturing draft at `/admin`, save it without changing the public version, and publish it after review. Draft saves and publication are included in the audit history. Uploaded manufacturing media must be company-approved: JPEG/PNG/WebP images up to 5 MB and MP4/WebM video up to 30 MB. Images may be assigned to the hero or a production stage; hero video is muted and loops. Uploaded assets are stored in the configured data directory with profile portraits. Back up that directory and use persistent storage in production.

No approved facility photographs or video were provided for this implementation. Until the company supplies approved material and confirms its use, the public experience uses conceptual 3D scenes and does not imply they depict the actual Chalinze facility. Review the process-route, capacity, product-dimension, source-image and project-status caveats below before publishing any content changes.

## Conflicts and unsupported claims withheld

1. **Capacity:** The project narrative describes Phase 1 as 1,000,000 tonnes per annum, followed by another 1,000,000 tonnes per annum in a later expansion. An earlier process diagram gives 1.2 million tonnes per annum. No single capacity is asserted.
2. **Ironmaking:** The main narrative describes natural-gas direct reduced iron (DRI); another process diagram says electric ironmaking. No selected route is asserted.
3. **Address:** The two headquarters descriptions differ. Both the conflict and requested verification are disclosed on Contact.
4. **Location:** Vigwaza Dry Port/area is a planned project location and not the Dar es Salaam office. Confirm the exact site boundary and pin.
5. **Status:** The design reference is not evidence of an operating facility. No production, construction, commissioning, availability or secured project-milestone status is claimed.
6. **Products:** Billets are the planned principal product. Reinforcement bars, coils and sheets shown in the design reference are not represented as direct company products. Reinforcement imagery appears only as a labelled downstream illustration.
7. **Certifications and capacity claims:** Unsupported reference-only ISO/certification statements and placeholder capacity figures are not repeated.
8. **Brand wording:** “Low-carbon” appears as wording in the approved mark and company ambition; no measured carbon intensity, certification or verified emissions reduction is claimed.
9. **Contact details:** The unverified sales email and placeholder street address shown in the design reference are excluded from the approved contact list.

## Technical and publication audit record

- Removed the unverified `sales@chalinzesteelmills.co.tz` address from the public contact page and replaced the site footer's review/development copy with a factual project description. Kept project-status and technical uncertainty notices where they prevent unsupported claims.
- Removed an unused legacy leadership seed module that marked invented placeholder biographies as Published. The active database seed and migration now preserve supplied biographies for review, move Denis and Juliette out of Board/director classifications, and require approval before public display.
- Added `admin` and `editor` roles with server-side publication/deletion checks. Existing administrator databases gain the role field through an idempotent migration.
- Leadership portraits and manufacturing images are decoded, orientation-corrected, resized to at most 1800 × 1800 and converted to WebP for delivery. Unmodified image originals are stored outside the static upload directory. Remote portrait URLs and malformed image payloads are rejected.
- Added route-specific canonical/Open Graph metadata, `noindex` for administration, a sitemap and robots directives.
- Responsive browser testing found that the leadership 3D-scene controls could be clipped at narrow mobile widths because the scene is offset beyond the viewport. The mobile controls are now anchored to the viewport's left content edge.
- Automated integration tests cover pending-profile visibility, source-record migration, role authorization, profile create/edit/publish/unpublish, image upload/replacement and optimization, malformed image rejection, manufacturing draft/public separation, publication, audit history, malformed cookies and cross-origin state-changing requests.
- No production deployment or external-host verification has been performed. The hosting platform, persistent storage, HTTPS termination, final DNS and production Node.js image-processing runtime still require deployment-side verification.
